Trends over time

Track a metric across every logged test date — team average, or individual athletes side by side.

Power profile: jump height × ground contact time

Every athlete's pogo-hop RSI test gives us three numbers from the same rep: how high they jump, how long they're on the ground, and RSI (jump height ÷ ground contact time). Plotting jump height against ground contact time sorts the roster into four training-need groups — who needs raw power, who needs to get off the ground faster, and who's already doing both well.

Speed profile: 40-yard dash × MAS (Bronco)

The 40-yard dash captures acceleration and top-end speed; MAS (maximal aerobic speed, derived from the Bronco: 1200m ÷ time) captures the aerobic engine that lets speed repeat over a game. Plotting them together separates true all-around speed from a big engine with no top gear, or a fast first step that can't be repeated.

Strength profile: relative squat × clean:squat ratio

Back squat relative to bodyweight captures maximal strength; the power clean : squat ratio captures how much of that strength converts into rate of force development. Plotting them together separates athletes who are strong but slow to apply it from athletes who are explosive relative to a still-developing strength base.
